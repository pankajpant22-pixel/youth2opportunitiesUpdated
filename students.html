FAQ

Questions students ask

Still wondering? info@youth2opportunities.ca

Is it free?+

Yes. Building and sharing your record is free for students.

What can I add?+

Work, volunteering, clubs, sports, competitions, side projects, community roles, and skills.

What if nobody can confirm something?+

Log it anyway. It stays on your record. Confirmed activities just carry more weight.

Do I have to share everything?+

No. Your record is private until you choose to share it, and you pick what goes in.

Is Grade 12 too late to start?+

No. Log what you've already done and ask people to confirm it. Starting earlier just makes it easier.

Start in Grade 9

Start before the record is needed

Grade 9 is ideal. Any grade works.
