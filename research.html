The shift

Grades compress, so the record has to do more

When top grades all look the same, what students did beyond class carries the weight.

Twenty years ago the average admitted student at UBC had 70%. Today it's 87%. The same shift shows up at most major Canadian universities.

When the top quarter of grades all look the same, the record has to separate them.

Universities now adjust grades by school and look harder at confirmed work. Employers do the same. The record has to start earlier.
