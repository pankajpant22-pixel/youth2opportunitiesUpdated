FAQ

Questions families ask

Still wondering? info@youth2opportunities.ca

Do I need to create an account?+

No. Your teen sends you a secure link to their record. Open it and you're in.

Can I edit my teen's record?+

No. The family view is read-only. Only your teen adds or removes activities, which keeps the record credible.

Is it only about the 40 volunteer hours?+

No. The 40 hours are part of it, but Y2O covers jobs, sports, clubs, caregiving, and everything else your teen puts effort into.

What if my teen hasn't done much yet?+

That's the best time to start. Seeing the record early helps them choose what to try next.

Is my teen's data sold?+

No. Student data is never sold.
