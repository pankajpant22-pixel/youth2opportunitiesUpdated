FAQ

Questions people ask

Still wondering? info@youth2opportunities.ca

Is Y2O live?+

Yes. More than 50 students across Ontario are building their records now, and the opportunities feed is open.

Is it free?+

Free for students. Free for organizations to confirm work and post opportunities.

Is this only for volunteer hours?+

No. Work, clubs, sports, side projects, community, and skills all count.

Who confirms an activity?+

The person who saw the work: a coach, employer, teacher, or organizer. One tap, no account needed.

Does Y2O replace transcripts?+

No. Grades still matter. Y2O adds the confirmed record of everything beyond them.
